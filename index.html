Learn Presenting to a Board with Phoebe

Numbers in the boardroom: what to show, what to withhold, and the questions you will be asked. Six sessions for data and AI leaders who get a slot at a board or a board committee: what a board is for and what its directors answer for, the paper and the pack, which numbers belong and how to withhold detail without ever withholding the truth, a bench that recolours real public data under the rules boards use, the questions that follow, and the ten minutes themselves. One constructed running case, a logistics company's head of data asking her board to approve a customer-data platform.

The six sessions 🧭 · from the board's job to the minutes

The running case is Fernhollow Freight, a constructed mid-size logistics company, continued from Stakeholder Management and Executive Briefings with the same people. The CEO has approved Sunniva Holm's shared customer record; the spend is above her delegated limit, so the board has to decide, and Sunniva has ten minutes on the agenda in front of a chair, an audit and risk chair and a non-executive who knows depots. Fernhollow and everyone in it are invented for teaching, and every page says so.

🏛️ Session 1 · foundations

What a board is for

Oversight, not management; the duties in the Companies Act and the governance code that sit behind every director's question; and whether your item is for decision, discussion or noting.

▶ Start here 📑 Session 2 · core

The board paper and the pack

The cover sheet, the executive summary and the appendix; what the FRC guidance asks of a board paper; and when the pack has to reach the directors.

the paper 🔢 Session 3 · core

Which numbers belong

KPI or operational detail, trend against target and tolerance, materiality and risk appetite, and the line between withholding detail and withholding the truth.

three numbers, not thirty 🚦 Session 4 · bench night

The RAG rule bench

Thirty real months of NHS England's four-hour A&E figure, recoloured under six rules in your browser: 0 greens or 21, the green months that were falling, and what one month hides.

★ the bench ❓ Session 5 · deeper

The questions you will be asked

Assurance, challenge and strategic questions, anticipated from your own pack; answering "I don't know" well and following up in writing; and pre-wiring the chair.

five questions, answered ⏳ Session 6 · deeper

The ten-minute slot, and after

Speak to the paper, do not read it; the minutes and the action log; following up. Capstone: Sunniva's slot assembled, from cover sheet to five expected questions.

What is honest here 🧾

Boardroom advice travels with famous numbers and sayings, and several have no source behind them. Each claim on these pages carries its status.

ClaimStatus
Every red, amber and green on the bench, and every count of themReally computed in your browser from NHS England's published monthly rows, under a rule printed on the widget
The tolerance band, the trend threshold and the trend windowConventions, chosen and labelled; no standard defines a RAG rule
Directors' duties and the governance codeRead at source: Companies Act 2006 sections 172 to 174, the UK Corporate Governance Code 2024, the G20/OECD Principles 2023
"Board packs average N pages", "directors spend X hours reading the pack"Reported: vendor and survey figures, quoted with who reported them; two surveys in 2024 and 2026 disagree on whether packs work
"Never bring a problem without a solution"Opinion, no traceable origin; the FRC's guidance warns against bringing matters for sign-off rather than debate
